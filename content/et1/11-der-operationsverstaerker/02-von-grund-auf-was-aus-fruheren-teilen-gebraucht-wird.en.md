This chapter calculates with potentials $\varphi$ with respect to ground ([Chapter 1](/wissen/en/elektrotechnik/1-semester/grundlagen-der-elektrotechnik/ladung-feld-und-potenzial/ladung-feld-und-potenzial.html)) and the node rule: the currents into a node sum to zero ([Chapter 2](/wissen/en/elektrotechnik/1-semester/grundlagen-der-elektrotechnik/strom-widerstand-und-kirchhoff/strom-widerstand-und-kirchhoff.html)). Two resistors in series divide a voltage in the ratio of their values (__voltage divider__, [Chapter 2](/wissen/en/elektrotechnik/1-semester/grundlagen-der-elektrotechnik/strom-widerstand-und-kirchhoff/strom-widerstand-und-kirchhoff.html)). For the capacitor, $i=C\,\mathrm{d} u/\mathrm{d} t$ holds ([Chapter 3](/wissen/en/elektrotechnik/1-semester/grundlagen-der-elektrotechnik/der-kondensator/der-kondensator.html)); for the diode in the forward direction, $I=I_\mathrm{S}\,\mathrm{e}^{U/U_T}$ ([Chapter 9](/wissen/en/elektrotechnik/1-semester/grundlagen-der-elektrotechnik/halbleiter-und-dioden/halbleiter-und-dioden.html)). A bipolar transistor in the active region is a voltage-controlled current source: $I_\mathrm{C}=I_\mathrm{S}\,\mathrm{e}^{U_\mathrm{BE}/U_T}$, hardly dependent on $U_\mathrm{CE}$. Around the operating point, the __transconductance__ $S=I_\mathrm{C}/U_T$ is the slope of this curve ([Chapter 10](/wissen/en/elektrotechnik/1-semester/grundlagen-der-elektrotechnik/der-bipolartransistor/der-bipolartransistor.html)). The rms values from [Chapter 7](/wissen/en/elektrotechnik/1-semester/grundlagen-der-elektrotechnik/leistung-anpassung-und-wirkungsgrad/leistung-und-anpassung.html) measure the strength of an alternating voltage.
