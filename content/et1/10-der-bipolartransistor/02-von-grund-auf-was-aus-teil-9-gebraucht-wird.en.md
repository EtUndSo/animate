In n-doped silicon, electrons are free to move; in p-doped silicon, __holes__ are: missing electrons that move like positive charges. At the boundary between the two regions, the __pn junction__, the __space-charge region__ forms: a strip with almost no free carriers, in which fixed ions produce an electric field from the n region to the p region. This field acts as a barrier for the majority carriers of each side. Plus at the p region lowers it, and the diode conducts in the __forward direction__. Plus at the n region raises it, and the diode blocks. __Diffusion__ is the motion of particles from a dense place to a sparse one; __recombination__ is the encounter of an electron with a hole, in which both disappear as free carriers. For $U\gg U_T$ the diode follows the equation $I=I_\mathrm{S}\,\mathrm{e}^{U/U_T}$, and at the operating point one calculates with the tangent to its characteristic curve.
