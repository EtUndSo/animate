Sets, the symbols $\in$, $\subset$ and the number systems are known from [Part 1](/wissen/en/elektrotechnik/1-semester/hohere-mathematik-1/mengen-logik-und-reelle-zahlen/mengen-logik-und-reelle-zahlen.html). Two building blocks are added. The __Cartesian product__ (also cross product) of two sets is the set of all ordered pairs:

$$A\times B:=\bigl\{(a,b):a\in A\ \wedge\ b\in B\bigr\}.$$

The order counts, $(1,2)\neq(2,1)$. For $n$ sets likewise $A_1\times\dots\times A_n:=\{(a_1,\dots,a_n):a_i\in A_i\text{ for all }i\}$, and $A^n:=A\times\dots\times A$ with $n$ factors. $\mathbb{R}^2$ is the number plane.

__Intervals__ are the connected pieces of the number line. For $a\le b$ the bounded sets $(a,b):=\{x\in\mathbb{R}:a<x<b\}$ are called __open__, $[a,b]:=\{x\in\mathbb{R}:a\le x\le b\}$ __closed__, and $(a,b]$ and $[a,b)$ __half-open__. The square bracket means: The endpoint belongs to the interval; the round one means: It does not. Unbounded are $(a,\infty):=\{x\in\mathbb{R}:a<x\}$, $[a,\infty):=\{x\in\mathbb{R}:a\le x\}$, $(-\infty,b):=\{x\in\mathbb{R}:x<b\}$, $(-\infty,b]:=\{x\in\mathbb{R}:x\le b\}$ and $(-\infty,\infty):=\mathbb{R}$. The symbol $\infty$ is not a number, which is why the round bracket always stands there. In addition, $\mathbb{R}_0^+:=[0,\infty)$.
