The answer in three sentences

  1. A periodic signal is a sum of oscillations at multiples of the fundamental frequency (Fourier series); a non-repeating signal is an integral over all frequencies (Fourier integral).

  2. Every oscillation $\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}2\pi ft}$ is an eigenfunction: an LTI system multiplies it by $H(f)$, and that is why convolution becomes the product $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G(f)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{S(f)\,H(f)}$.

  3. All the rules of calculation (shift, stretching, modulation, symmetry) follow from what the respective operation does to a single oscillation.

Key points

  • $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}2\pi ft}*h}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{H(f)\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}2\pi ft}}$; $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{H(f)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\int h\,\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{j}2\pi ft}\mathrm{d} t}$.

  • Series: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sum S_p(k)\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}2\pi kFt}}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{S_p(k)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac1T\int_Ts_p\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{j}2\pi kFt}\mathrm{d} t}$; real: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{S_p(-k)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{S_p^*(k)}$.

  • Parseval: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\frac1T\int_T|s_p|^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sum|S_p(k)|^2}$; Gibbs: about 9 % overshoot at jumps; LTI: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G_p(k)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{S_p(k)H(kF)}$.

  • $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{S(f)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\int s\,\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{j}2\pi ft}\mathrm{d} t}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\int S\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}2\pi ft}\mathrm{d} f}$; $s*h\leftrightarrow SH$, $s_1s_2\leftrightarrow S_1*S_2$.

  • Real: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{S(-f)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{S^*(f)}$; even $\leftrightarrow\operatorname{Re} S$, odd $\leftrightarrow\mathrm{j}\operatorname{Im} S$.

  • $s(bt)\leftrightarrow\frac1{|b|}S(f/b)$; $s(t-t_0)\leftrightarrow S\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{j}2\pi ft_0}$; $\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d} t}\leftrightarrow\mathrm{j}2\pi f$; $\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}2\pi Ft}s\leftrightarrow S(f-F)$; $S(t)\leftrightarrow s(-f)$.

  • $\operatorname{rect}(t/T)\leftrightarrow|T|\operatorname{si}(\pi Tf)$; $\Lambda\leftrightarrow\operatorname{si}^2$; $\delta\leftrightarrow1$; $1\leftrightarrow\delta(f)$; $\cos\leftrightarrow\frac12[\delta(f+F)+\delta(f-F)]$.

  • Comb $\leftrightarrow$ comb: $\sum\delta(t-nT)\leftrightarrow\frac1{|T|}\sum\delta(f-k/T)$; $\varepsilon\leftrightarrow\frac12\delta(f)+\frac{1}{\mathrm{j}2\pi f}$.

  • Hilbert: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{h}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{1}{\pi t}}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{H}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-\mathrm{j}\operatorname{sgn}(f)}$; $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_+}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac12(s+\mathrm{j}\hat s)}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2\operatorname{Re} s_+}$.
