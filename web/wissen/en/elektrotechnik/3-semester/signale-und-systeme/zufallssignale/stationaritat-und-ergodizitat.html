Stage 2: Stationarity and ergodicity

What this stage is about:

You never have an infinite ensemble, usually only a single signal. When is that enough?

A process is called stationary if none of its averages change when all observation times are shifted by the same time $t_0$:

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathrm E\{F[s(t_1)]\}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathrm E\{F[s(t_1+t_0)]\},}$$
$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathrm E\{F[s(t_1),s(t_2)]\}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathrm E\{F[s(t_1+t_0),s(t_2+t_0)]\}.}$$
(2.1)

Then the mean value $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{m_s}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathrm E\{s(t)\}}$ is a constant, and the ACF depends only on the difference $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\tau}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{t_2-t_1}$: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\varphi_{ss}(\tau)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathrm E\{s(t)s(t+\tau)\}}$. A stationary process is called ergodic if, in addition, every time average of a sample function equals the corresponding ensemble average:

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathrm E\{F[s(t)]\}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\overline{F[{}^ks(t)]}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\lim_{T\to\infty}\frac{1}{2T}\int_{-T}^{T}F\bigl[{}^ks(t)\bigr]\,\mathrm{d} t\quad\text{for every }k.}$$
(2.2)

⁠How to read it: Stationary means that the statistics do not change with time. Ergodic means that a single sample function, observed for a long time, shows in the course of time everything that the whole ensemble shows. The noise of a resistor at constant temperature is ergodic. Only for this reason can noise be characterized with a single measuring instrument.

QuestionIs every stationary process also ergodic?

No. Example: each sample function is a constant whose value is drawn at random once, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{{}^ks(t)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{c_k}$. The process is stationary, because nothing depends on $t$. But the time average of a sample function is $c_k$, which is different for every sample function and is not the ensemble average. A single sample function never “sees” the other values.

Summary

Terms
  • stationary – A process is called stationary if none of its averages change when all observation times are shifted by the same time $t_0$.
  • ergodic – A stationary process is called ergodic if, in addition, every time average of a sample function equals the corresponding ensemble average.
