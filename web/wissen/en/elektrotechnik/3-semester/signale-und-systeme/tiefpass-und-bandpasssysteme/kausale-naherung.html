Stage 4: Causal approximation

What this stage is about:

How do you build something that comes as close as possible to the ideal lowpass? You delay the impulse response and cut off the rest.

The idea. Shift $h(t)$ to the right by $t_0$ until the part at $t<0$ is negligible, and cut that part off. The envelope of the si function falls off like $1/(2\pi f_\mathrm g|t|)$. If it is to stay below 1 % of the maximum before $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$, you need $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{t_0}\textcolor{#15633a}{\approx}\textcolor{#c2410c}{16/f_\mathrm g}$. With a symmetric window of width $2t_0$:

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{h_k(t)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2f_\mathrm g\operatorname{si}\bigl(2\pi f_\mathrm g(t-t_0)\bigr)\operatorname{rect}\Bigl(\frac{t-t_0}{2t_0}\Bigr)\;\circ\!\!-\!\!\bullet\;H_k(f)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\Bigl[\operatorname{rect}\Bigl(\frac{f}{2f_\mathrm g}\Bigr)*2t_0\operatorname{si}(2\pi t_0f)\Bigr]\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{j}2\pi t_0f}.}$$
(4.1)

⁠How to read it: cutting off is a multiplication by a window, and so in the spectrum a convolution with the window's si spectrum. The sharp edge is smeared out and acquires ripples. The factor $\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{j}2\pi t_0f}$ is the delay: the causal filter has a linear phase; it delays all frequencies equally (Fig. 2). The larger $t_0$, the narrower the transition, but the later the response.

Exercise 4 ★★ How much delay does causality cost?

A lowpass with $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{f_\mathrm g}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1}$ kHz is to be approximated causally. The shifted ideal lowpass response is to stay below 1 % of its maximum before $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$, that is, in the part that is cut off.

  1. What is the minimum value of $t_0$?

  2. What does this mean for a telephone call through a chain of ten such filters?

Solution

(a) The envelope condition $\frac{1}{2\pi f_\mathrm g|t|}<0.01$ requires $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{|t|>\frac{100}{2\pi f_\mathrm g}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{15.9}$ ms. So $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{t_0}\textcolor{#15633a}{\approx}\textcolor{#c2410c}{16}$ ms.

(b) Ten filters delay by about 0.16 s. In a conversation this is noticeable as a delay. The causality of a steep filter costs time, and the narrower the band, the more time it costs.
