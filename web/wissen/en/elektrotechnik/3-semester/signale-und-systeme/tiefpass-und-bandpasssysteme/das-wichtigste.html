The answer in three sentences

  1. Undistorted means: only amplified and delayed. This requires a constant magnitude and a phase that falls linearly with frequency.

  2. A sharply band-limited (ideal) lowpass is not causal, overshoots by about 9 % at steps and needs a rise time of $1/(2f_\mathrm g)$. Softer edges reduce the overshoot at the expense of sharpness.

  3. A bandpass signal is a lowpass signal (the complex envelope) on a carrier. You calculate with the envelope as with a lowpass signal: it is delayed by the group delay, the carrier by the phase delay.

Key points

  • Distortionless: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{h}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{h_0\delta(t-t_0)}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{H}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{h_0\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{j}2\pi t_0f}}$: constant magnitude, linear phase.

  • $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{a}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-20\lg|H|}$ dB; $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{t_\mathrm p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-\frac{\varphi}{2\pi f}}$; $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{t_\mathrm g}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-\frac{1}{2\pi}\frac{\mathrm{d}\varphi}{\mathrm{d} f}}$.

  • Ideal lowpass: $\operatorname{rect}(f/2f_\mathrm g)\leftrightarrow2f_\mathrm g\operatorname{si}(2\pi f_\mathrm gt)$, not causal; $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{f_\mathrm g t_m}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac12}$; overshoot 21.7 % (impulse), 8.95 % (step).

  • Step response $\frac12+\frac1\pi\operatorname{Si}(2\pi f_\mathrm gt)$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\operatorname{Si}(\infty)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac\pi2}$, rise time $\frac{1}{2f_\mathrm g}$.

  • Truncating the impulse response $=$ convolution in the spectrum; smooth windows: deeper stopband, shallower edge.

  • Pulse-shaping filter: $T\operatorname{rect}(Tf)[1+\cos(2\pi Tf)]$, little overshoot.

  • $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{H(f)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac12H_T(f-f_0)+\frac12H_T^*(-f-f_0)}$; $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\operatorname{Re}\{s_T\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}2\pi f_0t}\}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s_{Tr}\cos-s_{Ti}\sin}$; $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_T}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2s_+\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{j}2\pi f_0t}}$.

  • $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{g_T}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac12s_T*h_T}$; envelope delayed by $t_\mathrm g$, carrier by $t_\mathrm p$.

  • Bandpass sampling: $2\Delta f$ values per second; digital highpass: $(-1)^nh_{\mathrm{TP}}(n)$.
