The answer in three sentences

  1. For orthonormal basis functions, the best coefficient of a function is its correlation with the basis function, and the energy is the sum of the squared coefficients.

  2. On a line, every voltage is a superposition of a forward and a backward wave. The line is described by the propagation constant $\gamma$ and the characteristic impedance $Z$.

  3. If the line is terminated with its characteristic impedance, the backward wave vanishes. On a lossless line all frequencies travel equally fast, and signals arrive undistorted.

Key points

  • Orthonormal: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\int s_is_j\mathrm{d} t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\delta_{ij}}$; best coefficients $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{b_i}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\int fs_i\mathrm{d} t}$; $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{E_r}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{E_f-\sum b_i^2}$; complete: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{E_f}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sum b_i^2}$.

  • Transmission-line equations: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\partial_su}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-(R'+L'\partial_t)i}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\partial_si}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-(G'+C'\partial_t)u}$.

  • Phasors (with $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\omega}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2\pi f}$): $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\underline U}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\underline U_1\mathrm{e}^{-\gamma s}+\underline U_2\mathrm{e}^{\gamma s}}$; $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\gamma}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sqrt{(R'+\mathrm{j}\omega L')(G'+\mathrm{j}\omega C')}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\alpha+\mathrm{j}\beta}$; $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sqrt{\frac{R'+\mathrm{j}\omega L'}{G'+\mathrm{j}\omega C'}}}$.

  • Termination with $Z$: no reflection; $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{r}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{R_\mathrm E-Z}{R_\mathrm E+Z}}$.

  • Lossless: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sqrt{L'/C'}}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{v}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1/\sqrt{L'C'}}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{u}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{u_\mathrm{fw}(s-vt)+u_\mathrm{bw}(s+vt)}$.
