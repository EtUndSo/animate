The answer in three sentences

  1. At the pn junction, mobile charges move into the other region and leave fixed ions behind. The field of these ions builds up a barrier in the space-charge region that stops the equalization.

  2. Heat lifts only the fraction $\mathrm{e}^{-W/(k_\mathrm BT)}$ of the charge carriers over a barrier of height $W$. Voltage in the forward direction lowers the barrier, so the current grows exponentially, by a factor of 10 per 60 mV: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{I}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{I_\mathrm S(\mathrm{e}^{U/U_T}-1)}$. In the reverse direction only the tiny current $-I_\mathrm S$ remains.

  3. In a circuit, the operating point lies at the intersection of the characteristic curve and the load line (the straight line set by source and resistor). For hand calculations the diode becomes a switch with a fixed forward voltage, and for small changes around the operating point it becomes a resistor $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{r}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{U_T/I}$.

Key points

  • Bands: $W_\mathrm g$ (silicon 1.12 eV), fraction $\mathrm{e}^{-W/(k_\mathrm BT)}$; the carriers are electrons in the CB and holes in the VB.

  • Doping: donors give $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{n}\textcolor{#15633a}{\approx}\textcolor{#c2410c}{ N_\mathrm D}$, acceptors $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{p}\textcolor{#15633a}{\approx}\textcolor{#c2410c}{ N_\mathrm A}$, everywhere $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{n\,p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{n_\mathrm i^2}$. The crystal stays neutral.

  • pn junction: diffusion, recombination, fixed ions, field. $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_\mathrm{D0}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{U_T\ln(N_\mathrm AN_\mathrm D/n_\mathrm i^2)}$.

  • Characteristic curve: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{I}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{I_\mathrm S(\mathrm{e}^{U/U_T}-1)}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_T}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{k_\mathrm BT/e}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{25.85}$ mV at 300 K, 60 mV per decade, no threshold.

  • Breakdown is reversible as long as the heat stays limited. Zener diode: $U_\mathrm Z$, $r_\mathrm Z$. Varactor diode: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{C_\mathrm{j}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\varepsilon A/w}$. Photodiode: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{I}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{I_\mathrm S(\mathrm{e}^{U/U_T}-1)-I_\mathrm{Ph}}$, solar cell $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_\mathrm L}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{U_T\ln(I_\mathrm{Ph}/I_\mathrm S+1)}$.

  • Operating point: intersection of the characteristic curve and the load line $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{I}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{(U_0-U_\mathrm D)/R}$; iterate via $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_\mathrm D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{U_T\ln(I/I_\mathrm S+1)}$.

  • Models: conducting $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_\mathrm D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ or $U_\mathrm F$, blocking $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{I_\mathrm D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$. Assume, calculate, check: $I_\mathrm D>0$ and $U_\mathrm D<U_\mathrm F$.

  • Small signal: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{r}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{U_T/I}$ for $|u_\mathrm D|\ll U_T$. Do not confuse with $U_\mathrm D/I$.

  • Bridge: peak $\hat u-2U_\mathrm F$, gap at $|u_\mathrm e|<2U_\mathrm F$. Charge pump: $U_\mathrm a\to2U_\mathrm e-2U_\mathrm F$.

One principle behind all the results. Heat distributes energy at random, and the fraction of particles above an energy falls exponentially with this energy. Almost everything in this chapter follows from that, once you ask which energy counts in each case.
