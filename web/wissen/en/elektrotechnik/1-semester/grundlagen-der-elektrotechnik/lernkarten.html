  1. Termvoltage

Chapter 2: Current, Resistance and Kirchhoff's Laws

  1. Termnode rule
    The node rule says that no current disappears at a junction.
    Overview
  2. Termmesh rule
    The mesh rule says that the voltages along a closed path add up to zero.
    Overview
  3. Termcharge density
    The charge density $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{q}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\Delta Q/\Delta V}$ is the charge per volume.
    Stage 1: Current is moving charge
  4. Termelectric current
    The electric current (current for short) is the charge that passes through an area per time.
    Stage 1: Current is moving charge
  5. Termcircuit
    Second, the circuit must be closed, that is, a path that returns to itself.
    Stage 1: Current is moving charge
  6. Termsteady
    A current that is constant in time in a closed circuit is called steady.
    Stage 1: Current is moving charge

    The positive ions left behind form a regular crystal lattice.

    $\mu$ is the mobility, the drift velocity per field strength, in $\mathrm{m^2/(V\,s)}$.
    Stage 2: Slow electrons, immediate current

    Stage 4: Real resistors: values and temperature
