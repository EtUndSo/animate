The answer in three sentences

  1. Inside sits a differential amplifier, a circuit that responds only to the difference between two voltages: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_\mathrm{A}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{A\,U_\mathrm{D}}$ with $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_\mathrm{D}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\varphi_+-\varphi_-}$. With $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{A}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{100000}$ and at most 11 V at the output, this leaves a window of $\pm110$ µ V for $U_\mathrm{D}$.

  2. Negative feedback routes the fraction $\beta$ of the output to the minus input. The output runs until the loop comes to rest, and that happens when $U_\mathrm{D}$ is just enough for the output: 5 V need 50 µ V. The gain becomes $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G}\textcolor{#15633a}{\approx}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1/\beta}$, and the loop gain $A\beta$ (the factor for one trip around the loop) only sets the small deviation.

  3. This yields a rule: equal potentials at the inputs, no current into them, node rule at the minus input. It explains the virtual ground (a node at 0 V with no connection to ground) and all the basic circuits. It ends where the output hits its limit, where the feedback changes sign, and where $A\beta$ becomes small.

Key points

  • Noise: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{u_\mathrm{r,eff}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sqrt{\int|u_\mathrm{r}(f)|^2\mathrm{d} f}}$, thermal $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{|u_\mathrm{r,R}|^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{4k_\mathrm{B}TR}$. Amplifying does not change the SNR; it helps only against later disturbances. $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathrm{SNR}_\mathrm{dB}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{20\lg(u_\mathrm{s}/u_\mathrm{r})}$.

  • Differential amplifier: fixed total current, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_\mathrm{A}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{S\,R_\mathrm{C}\,U_\mathrm{D}}$, each BE junction carries $U_\mathrm{D}/2$, common mode has no effect.

  • Model: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_\mathrm{A}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{A(\varphi_+-\varphi_-)}$ up to the limits. Window $\pm110$ µ V, comparator without feedback.

  • Negative feedback: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_\mathrm{D}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{U_\mathrm{E}/(1+A\beta)}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac1\beta\cdot\frac1{1+1/(A\beta)}}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\Delta G/G}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{1}{1+A\beta}\Delta A/A}$. The output runs until the loop rests. At 5 V, 50 µ V remains.

  • Rule: $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\varphi_+}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\varphi_-}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{I_\pm}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$, node rule at the minus input, check the limits last. Virtual ground: 0 V through regulation, no path to ground. The current runs through the feedback into the output.

  • $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-R_2/R_1}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1+R_2/R_1}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_\mathrm{A}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-R_\mathrm{F}\sum U_{\mathrm{E}i}/R_{\mathrm{E}i}}$, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_\mathrm{A}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{R_2}{R_1}(U_\mathrm{E2}-U_\mathrm{E1})}$, integrator $-\frac{1}{RC}\int u_\mathrm{E}\mathrm{d} t$, differentiator $-RC\,\mathrm{d} u_\mathrm{E}/\mathrm{d} t$, logarithmic amplifier $-U_T\ln(U_\mathrm{E}/RI_\mathrm{S})$, exponential amplifier $-RI_\mathrm{S}\mathrm{e}^{U_\mathrm{E}/U_T}$, transimpedance $-R\,I_\mathrm{E}$, NIC $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{R_\mathrm{in}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-R_2}$.

  • Positive feedback: the effect reinforces the cause, output at the limit, thresholds $\beta\,U_\mathrm{A,max}$ and $\beta\,U_\mathrm{A,min}$.

  • Limits: overdrive, $U_\mathrm{off}/\beta$, $I_\mathrm{B}R_2$, CMRR, $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G\,f_G}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{f_\mathrm{T}}$.

One principle behind all the results. The output of an op amp with negative feedback is not a calculation with $A$ but the state in which the loop comes to rest. For this, $A$ only has to be large; its exact value does not matter.
