第 8 阶段：复包络与正交分量

本阶段内容：

对系统成立的，对信号也成立：带通信号是搭载在载波上的低通信号。本阶段给出写出这一关系的几种不同方式。

用调制定理对式 (7.2) 作反变换，得到 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{h(t)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac12h_T(t)\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}2\pi f_0t}+\frac12\bigl[h_T(t)\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}2\pi f_0t}\bigr]^*}$，于是由 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z+z^*}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2\operatorname{Re} z}$ 得：

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{h(t)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\operatorname{Re}\bigl\{h_T(t)\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}2\pi f_0t}\bigr\},}$$
$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s(t)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\operatorname{Re}\bigl\{s_T(t)\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}2\pi f_0t}\bigr\}.}$$
(8.1)

$s_T(t)$ 称为复包络，$\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}2\pi f_0t}$ 称为复载波。二者乘积的一半就是第 3 章中的解析分量 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_+(t)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac12s_T(t)\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}2\pi f_0t}}$，因此 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_T}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2s_+\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{j}2\pi f_0t}}$。

定理 8.1 (正交表示)

利用 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_T}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s_{Tr}+\mathrm{j} s_{Ti}}$ 和希尔伯特变换 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\hat s}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s*\frac{1}{\pi t}}$：

$$\begin{aligned} s(t)&=s_{Tr}(t)\cos(2\pi f_0t)-s_{Ti}(t)\sin(2\pi f_0t),\\ s_{Tr}(t)&=s(t)\cos(2\pi f_0t)+\hat s(t)\sin(2\pi f_0t),\qquad s_{Ti}(t)=\hat s(t)\cos(2\pi f_0t)-s(t)\sin(2\pi f_0t),\\ s(t)&=|s_T(t)|\cos\bigl(2\pi f_0t+\varphi_T(t)\bigr),\qquad|s_T|=\sqrt{s_{Tr}^2+s_{Ti}^2}. \end{aligned}$$
(8.2)(8.3)(8.4)

⁠如何理解：$s_{Tr}$ 和 $s_{Ti}$ 合称正交分量（分别为同相分量和正交分量）：一个调制载波的余弦，另一个调制载波的正弦。$|s_T|$ 是包络，即在示波器上看到的信号“轮廓”；$\varphi_T$ 是载波的相位偏移。若复包络为实数（对称情形），则 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s(t)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s_T(t)\cos(2\pi f_0t)}$：一个调幅余弦。

推导: 正交分量

式 (8.2)：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\operatorname{Re}\{(s_{Tr}+\mathrm{j} s_{Ti})(\cos+\mathrm{j}\sin)\}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s_{Tr}\cos-s_{Ti}\sin}$。对于式 (8.3)：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{2s_+}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s+\mathrm{j}\hat s}$（第 3 章），因此

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_T}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{(s+\mathrm{j}\hat s)(\cos-\mathrm{j}\sin)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{(s\cos+\hat s\sin)+\mathrm{j}(\hat s\cos-s\sin).}$$

练习 8 ★★ 求复包络

相对于 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{f_0}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{10}$ Hz，求下列信号的 $s_T$、$s_{Tr}$、$s_{Ti}$ 和 $|s_T|$： (a) $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s(t)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\bigl[1+0.5\cos(2\pi\cdot1\,\mathrm{Hz}\cdot t)\bigr]\cos(2\pi\cdot10\,\mathrm{Hz}\cdot t)}$， (b) $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s(t)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\cos(2\pi\cdot10\,\mathrm{Hz}\cdot t+0.3)}$， (c) $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s(t)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sin(2\pi\cdot10\,\mathrm{Hz}\cdot t)}$。用式 (8.2) 验证 (c)。

解答

(a) $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_T}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1+0.5\cos(2\pi t)}$，为实数：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_{Tr}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s_T}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_{Ti}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{|s_T|}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1+0.5\cos(2\pi t)}$，在 0.5 与 1.5 之间。这是一个调幅余弦。

(b) $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_T}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}0.3}}$：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_{Tr}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\cos0.3}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0.955}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_{Ti}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sin0.3}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0.296}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{|s_T|}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1}$。包络是恒定的，只是相位移动了 0.3。

(c) $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\sin x}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\operatorname{Re}\{-\mathrm{j}\,\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j} x}\}}$，因此 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_T}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-\mathrm{j}}$：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_{Tr}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s_{Ti}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-1}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{|s_T|}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1}$。验证：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{0\cdot\cos-(-1)\sin}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sin}$。
