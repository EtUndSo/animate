第 2 阶段：度量相似程度——相关系数

DEENES中文
本阶段内容：

怎样用一个数来度量两个信号有多相似？办法是度量它们的差有多大。

思路。 两个信号相似时，差信号 $\textcolor{#3f1a78}{\Delta(t)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s(t)-g(t)}$ 的能量就小。展开得：

$$\textcolor{#3f1a78}{E_\Delta}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\int|s-g|^2\mathrm{d} t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{E_s+E_g-\int s^*g\,\mathrm{d} t-\int g^*s\,\mathrm{d} t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{E_s+E_g-2\operatorname{Re}\int s^*(t)g(t)\,\mathrm{d} t.}$$

因此，差别完全体现在乘积的积分中。为了不让信号本身的大小计入在内，把两个信号都归一化到能量 1。于是 $\textcolor{#3f1a78}{E_\Delta}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2-2\operatorname{Re}\,p_{sg}}$，其中相关系数为

$$\textcolor{#3f1a78}{p_{sg}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}s^*(t)\,g(t)\,\mathrm{d} t}{\sqrt{E_sE_g}}.}$$
(2.1)

⁠如何理解：$|p_{sg}|\le1$（柯西–施瓦茨不等式）。$\textcolor{#3f1a78}{p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1}$ 表示 $\textcolor{#3f1a78}{s}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{a\,g}$ 且 $a>0$：形状相同。$\textcolor{#3f1a78}{p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-1}$ 表示 $\textcolor{#3f1a78}{s}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-a\,g}$：形状相同，符号相反。$\textcolor{#3f1a78}{p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 表示 $\textcolor{#3f1a78}{\int s^*g\,\mathrm{d} t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$；这两个信号是正交的，就像第 3 部分中傅里叶级数的各次谐波一样。例：对于 $\operatorname{rect}(t)$ 和 $\Lambda(t)$，有 $\textcolor{#3f1a78}{\int\operatorname{rect}\cdot\Lambda\,\mathrm{d} t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0.75}$，$\textcolor{#3f1a78}{E_{\operatorname{rect}}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1}$，$\textcolor{#3f1a78}{E_\Lambda}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac23}$，因此 $\textcolor{#3f1a78}{p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0.919}$。

练习 2 ★★ 相关系数

计算下列各对信号的 $p$：(a) $\operatorname{rect}(t)$ 与 $\operatorname{rect}(t-0.5)$，(b) $\operatorname{rect}(t)$ 与 $\Lambda(t)$（结果见上文），(c) $\operatorname{rect}(t)$ 与 $\operatorname{sgn}(t)\operatorname{rect}(t)$。

解答

(a) 两个矩形在长度 0.5 上重叠，因此 $\textcolor{#3f1a78}{\int}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0.5}$，且两者的能量都是 1：$\textcolor{#3f1a78}{p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0.5}$。 (b) $\textcolor{#3f1a78}{\int\operatorname{rect}\cdot\Lambda\,\mathrm{d} t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}(1-|t|)\mathrm{d} t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0.75}$，$\textcolor{#3f1a78}{E_\Lambda}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2\int_0^1(1-t)^2\mathrm{d} t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac23}$：$\textcolor{#3f1a78}{p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{0.75}{\sqrt{2/3}}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0.919}$。形状非常相似。 (c) $\textcolor{#3f1a78}{\int\operatorname{sgn}(t)\operatorname{rect}^2(t)\mathrm{d} t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$：$\textcolor{#3f1a78}{p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$；这两个信号正交，尽管它们在右半部分完全相同。
