要点

  • $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{E_s}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\int|s|^2\mathrm{d} t}$；$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{L_s}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\lim\frac{1}{2T}\int_{-T}^T|s|^2\mathrm{d} t}$。

  • $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{p_{sg}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{\int s^*g\,\mathrm{d} t}{\sqrt{E_sE_g}}}$，$|p|\le1$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$：正交。

  • $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\varphi^E_{sg}(\tau)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\int s^*(t)g(t+\tau)\mathrm{d} t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s^*(-\tau)*g(\tau)}$；$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\varphi^E_{gs}(\tau)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\varphi^{E*}_{sg}(-\tau)}$。

  • 自相关函数：偶函数（对实信号），最大值 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\varphi^E_{ss}(0)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{E_s}$，持续时间加倍。

  • $\varphi^E_{ss}\leftrightarrow|S|^2$，$\varphi^E_{sg}\leftrightarrow S^*G$；帕塞瓦尔定理 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\int|s|^2\mathrm{d} t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\int|S|^2\mathrm{d} f}$。

  • 维纳–李关系：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\varphi^E_{gg}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\varphi^E_{ss}*\varphi^E_{hh}}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{|G|^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{|S|^2|H|^2}$；$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\varphi^E_{sg}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\varphi^E_{ss}*h}$。

  • 带通：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\varphi^E_{sgT}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac12s_T^*(-\tau)*g_T(\tau)}$。
