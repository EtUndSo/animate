本阶段内容：

目标原本是求 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{x^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-1}$ 的解。本阶段说明 $\mathbb{C}$ 能做到更多：在其中每个二次方程都有解，即使系数是复数。

基本思路。 配方只用到域公理，所以在 $\mathbb{C}$ 中也成立。最后剩下方程 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{D}$。在 $\mathbb{R}$ 中，它在 $D<0$ 时无解。在 $\mathbb{C}$ 中它总有解：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{D}$ 分解为实部和虚部，也就是关于 $w$ 的两个实未知量的两个实方程。

定理 6.1 ($\mathbb{C}$ 中的平方根)

设 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s+\mathrm{i}t\ne0}$，其中 $s,t\in\mathbb{R}$。对 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{u+\mathrm{i}v}$（$u,v$ 为实数），$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{D}$ 等价于 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{u^2-v^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s}$ 和 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{2uv}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{t}$。恰有两个解 $\pm w$，其中

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{u^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{|D|+s}2,}$$
$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{v^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{|D|-s}2.}$$
(6.1)

$u$ 和 $v$ 的符号要选得使 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{2uv}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{t}$ 成立。当 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 时，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 是唯一解。

⁠如何理解：$s$ 和 $t$ 是 $D$ 的实部和虚部。若 $t>0$，则 $u$ 和 $v$ 同号；若 $t<0$，则异号。极限情况：对实数 $D<0$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{u}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{v^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-D}$：解是纯虚数，当 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-1}$ 时即 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\pm\mathrm{i}}$。

证明: 两个实方程

有 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{u^2-v^2+2\mathrm{i}uv}$。比较实部和虚部，得到这两个方程。由 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{|w|^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{|w^2|}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{|D|}$ 还可得 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{u^2+v^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{|D|}$。与 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{u^2-v^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s}$ 相加、相减，得式 (6.1)。等式右边非负，因为 $|s|\le|D|$（第 5 阶段，规则 (v)）。反过来，由式 (6.1) 得到的 $u,v$ 满足 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{u^2-v^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s}$ 和 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{(2uv)^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{(|D|+s)(|D|-s)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{|D|^2-s^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{t^2}$。所以 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{2uv}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\pm t}$，而符号的选择确定 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{2uv}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{t}$。

定理 6.2 ($\mathbb{C}$ 中的二次方程)

设 $p,q\in\mathbb{C}$，$D:=p^2/4-q$。方程 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z^2+pz+q}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 的解为

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-\frac p2\pm w}$$
$$\text{其中}$$
$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{D.}$$
(6.2)

当 $D\ne0$ 时，这是两个解 $z_+\ne z_-$，满足 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z_++z_-}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-p}$ 和 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z_+z_-}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{q}$。当 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 时，只有 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-p/2}$，即一个二重解。对实数 $p,q$：$D>0$ 给出两个实数解，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 给出一个实数解，$D<0$ 给出互相共轭的解 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-\frac p2\pm\mathrm{i}\sqrt{-D}}$。

⁠如何理解：$D$ 称为判别式。两个解关于中点 $-p/2$ 点对称（图 6）。适用条件：$z^2$ 的系数必须为 1。否则先除以它，在 $\mathbb{C}$ 中即使系数是不为零的复数也可以这样做。这类实系数方程描述振荡：一对共轭解对应一个阻尼振荡。

推导: 配方

有 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z^2+pz+q}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{(z+\frac p2)^2-\frac{p^2}4+q}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{(z+\frac p2)^2-w^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{(z+\frac p2-w)(z+\frac p2+w)}$。乘积为零只有在某个因子为零时才可能（第 4 阶段）。由此得式 (6.2)，并得 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z_++z_-}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-p}$ 和 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z_+z_-}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{p^2}4-w^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{q}$。当 $p,q$ 为实数时，$D$ 是实数，当 $D<0$ 时 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\pm\mathrm{i}\sqrt{-D}}$。

例 1实系数

$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z^2-6z+10}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 中 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-6}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{q}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{10}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{9-10}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-1}$，所以 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{3\pm\mathrm{i}}$。这就是 $z_1$ 和 $\overline{z_1}$。检验：和 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{6}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-p}$，积 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{|z_1|^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{10}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{q}$。

例 2复系数

对 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z^2-(4+3\mathrm{i})z+1+7\mathrm{i}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$，有 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{-p/2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2+1.5\mathrm{i}}$ 和

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{(2+1.5\mathrm{i})^2-(1+7\mathrm{i})}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1.75+6\mathrm{i}-1-7\mathrm{i}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0.75-\mathrm{i}.}$$

所以 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{s}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0.75}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-1}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{|D|}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sqrt{0.5625+1}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1.25}$。由式 (6.1) 得 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{u^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{v^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0.25}$。因为 $t<0$，$u$ 和 $v$ 异号：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\pm(1-0.5\mathrm{i})}$。于是 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2+1.5\mathrm{i}\pm(1-0.5\mathrm{i})}$，即 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{3+\mathrm{i}}$ 或 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1+2\mathrm{i}}$。检验：和 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{4+3\mathrm{i}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-p}$，积 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{1+7\mathrm{i}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{q}$。

⁠极限情况：当 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{q}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 时，式 (6.2) 给出 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-p/2\pm p/2}$，即 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 和 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-p}$，与 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z(z+p)}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 相符。

问题为什么不能这样算：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\sqrt{-1}\cdot\sqrt{-1}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sqrt{(-1)(-1)}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1}$？

第 1 章的法则 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\sqrt{ab}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sqrt a\sqrt b}$ 只对 $a,b\ge0$ 成立。对负数，根号没有定义：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-1}$ 有两个地位相同的解，其中哪一个都不是“那个”平方根。所以本章写 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{D}$，而不写 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sqrt D}$。

练习 8 ★★ 解二次方程

(a) 解 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z^2+4z+13}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$，并检验和与积。(b) 实系数方程 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z^2+pz+q}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 有解 $2-5\mathrm{i}$。第二个解是什么，$p$ 和 $q$ 等于多少？(c) 解 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z^2-3z+3-\mathrm{i}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 并检验。(d) 解 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathrm{i}z^2+2z-2\mathrm{i}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$。

解答

(a) $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{4-13}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-9}$，所以 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-2\pm3\mathrm{i}}$。和 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{-4}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-p}$，积 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{4+9}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{13}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{q}$。(b) 系数为实数时，第二个解是共轭解 $2+5\mathrm{i}$。于是 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-(2-5\mathrm{i})-(2+5\mathrm{i})}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-4}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{q}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{(2-5\mathrm{i})(2+5\mathrm{i})}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{29}$。

(c) $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-3}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{q}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{3-\mathrm{i}}$，所以 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2.25-3+\mathrm{i}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-0.75+\mathrm{i}}$。由 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{|D|}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\sqrt{0.5625+1}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1.25}$，式 (6.1) 给出 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{u^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0.25}$ 和 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{v^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1}$。因为 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{t}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1>0}$，$u$ 和 $v$ 同号：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\pm(0.5+\mathrm{i})}$。所以 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1.5\pm(0.5+\mathrm{i})}$，即 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2+\mathrm{i}}$ 或 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1-\mathrm{i}}$。检验：和 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{3}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-p}$，积 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{(2+\mathrm{i})(1-\mathrm{i})}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2-2\mathrm{i}+\mathrm{i}-\mathrm{i}^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{3-\mathrm{i}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{q}$。

(d) 先把 $z^2$ 的系数化为 1：用 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{1/\mathrm{i}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-\mathrm{i}}$ 除以 $\mathrm{i}$，得 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z^2-2\mathrm{i}z-2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$。于是 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{p}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-2\mathrm{i}}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{q}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-2}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{(-2\mathrm{i})^2/4+2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-1+2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1}$。由 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\pm1}$ 得 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathrm{i}\pm1}$。对 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1+\mathrm{i}}$ 检验：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathrm{i}\cdot2\mathrm{i}+2+2\mathrm{i}-2\mathrm{i}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-2+2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$。对 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-1+\mathrm{i}}$ 检验：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathrm{i}\cdot(-2\mathrm{i})-2+2\mathrm{i}-2\mathrm{i}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{2-2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$。

展望。 平面上的点也可以用绝对值和角度来表示。这样，做乘法时绝对值相乘、角度相加，第 2 阶段的旋转就是 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{|\mathrm{i}|}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1}$、角度为 $90^\circ$ 的特例。这种极坐标表示、指数形式和 $n$ 次方根需要正弦、余弦和指数函数，所以留到第 7 章。

定理 6.1 ($\mathbb{C}$ 中的平方根)

设 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s+\mathrm{i}t\ne0}$，其中 $s,t\in\mathbb{R}$。对 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{u+\mathrm{i}v}$（$u,v$ 为实数），$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{D}$ 等价于 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{u^2-v^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{s}$ 和 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{2uv}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{t}$。恰有两个解 $\pm w$，其中

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{u^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{|D|+s}2,}$$
$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{v^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{|D|-s}2.}$$
(6.1)

$u$ 和 $v$ 的符号要选得使 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{2uv}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{t}$ 成立。当 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 时，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 是唯一解。

定理 6.2 ($\mathbb{C}$ 中的二次方程)

设 $p,q\in\mathbb{C}$，$D:=p^2/4-q$。方程 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z^2+pz+q}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 的解为

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-\frac p2\pm w}$$
$$\text{其中}$$
$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{w^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{D.}$$
(6.2)

当 $D\ne0$ 时，这是两个解 $z_+\ne z_-$，满足 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z_++z_-}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-p}$ 和 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z_+z_-}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{q}$。当 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 时，只有 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-p/2}$，即一个二重解。对实数 $p,q$：$D>0$ 给出两个实数解，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 给出一个实数解，$D<0$ 给出互相共轭的解 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-\frac p2\pm\mathrm{i}\sqrt{-D}}$。
