第 6 阶段：换算、T 与 $\pi$

本阶段内容：

Z 形式和 Y 形式描述同一个二端口网络。本阶段说明如何从一种形式变到另一种形式，以及某种形式何时根本不存在。

基本思路。 Y 形式和 Z 形式是同样的两个方程，一个对电压求解，另一个对电流求解。因此转换就是解方程组，而这正是“从头开始”一节中的逆矩阵所做的事。

定理 6.1 (Z 与 Y 的换算)

电导矩阵和电阻矩阵互为逆矩阵，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathbf Y\cdot\mathbf Z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathbf 1}$：

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathbf Y}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathbf Z^{-1}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{1}{\det\mathbf Z}\begin{pmatrix}Z_{22}&-Z_{12}\\-Z_{21}&Z_{11}\end{pmatrix},}$$
$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathbf Z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathbf Y^{-1}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{1}{\det\mathbf Y}\begin{pmatrix}Y_{22}&-Y_{12}\\-Y_{21}&Y_{11}\end{pmatrix}}$$
(6.1)

其中 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\det\mathbf Z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{Z_{11}Z_{22}-Z_{12}Z_{21}}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\det\mathbf Y}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{Y_{11}Y_{22}-Y_{12}Y_{21}}$。

⁠如何理解：求逆的是整个矩阵，而不是逐个数取倒数。每个元素都带有整个分母，所以每个 Y 参数都取决于所有 Z 参数。适用条件：行列式不得为零。

推导: 求逆的两种途径

⁠用矩阵。由 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathbf I}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathbf Y\mathbf U}$ 和 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathbf U}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathbf Z\mathbf I}$ 得，对每个 $\mathbf I$ 都有 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathbf I}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathbf Y\mathbf Z\,\mathbf I}$，所以 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathbf Y\mathbf Z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathbf 1}$。

⁠在 Z 等效电路上用短路试验。$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 时，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Z_{21}I_1}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-Z_{22}I_2}$，所以 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{I_2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-\frac{Z_{21}}{Z_{22}}I_1}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_1}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\bigl(Z_{11}-Z_{12}\frac{Z_{21}}{Z_{22}}\bigr)I_1}$。于是

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Y_{11}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{I_1}{U_1}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{Z_{22}}{Z_{11}Z_{22}-Z_{12}Z_{21}},}$$
$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Y_{21}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{I_2}{U_1}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-\frac{Z_{21}}{Z_{11}Z_{22}-Z_{12}Z_{21}}.}$$

$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_1}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 时，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{-Z_{11}I_1}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{Z_{12}I_2}$，所以 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{I_1}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-\frac{Z_{12}}{Z_{11}}I_2}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\bigl(Z_{22}-Z_{21}\frac{Z_{12}}{Z_{11}}\bigr)I_2}$。由此得

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Y_{12}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-\frac{Z_{12}}{Z_{11}Z_{22}-Z_{12}Z_{21}},}$$
$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Y_{22}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{Z_{11}}{Z_{11}Z_{22}-Z_{12}Z_{21}}.}$$

T 与 $\pi$。 第 3 阶段 T 形网络的 Z 矩阵可用式 (6.1) 求逆，$\pi$ 形网络的 Y 矩阵可直接从短路试验读出。设 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{D}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{R_\mathrm{a}R_\mathrm{b}+R_\mathrm{a}R_\mathrm{c}+R_\mathrm{b}R_\mathrm{c}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\det\mathbf Z}$，并联电导为 $G_1$、$G_3$，串联电导为 $G_2$，则

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathbf Y_\mathrm{T}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac1D\begin{pmatrix}R_\mathrm{b}+R_\mathrm{c}&-R_\mathrm{b}\\-R_\mathrm{b}&R_\mathrm{a}+R_\mathrm{b}\end{pmatrix},}$$
$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathbf Y_\pi}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\begin{pmatrix}G_1+G_2&-G_2\\-G_2&G_2+G_3\end{pmatrix}.}$$
(6.2)

令两者相等，得 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G_2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-Y_{12}}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G_1}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{Y_{11}+Y_{12}}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G_3}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{Y_{22}+Y_{21}}$。$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G_3}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 时，$\mathbf Y_\pi$ 变为 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{I_1}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{(G_1+G_2)U_1-G_2U_2}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{I_2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-G_2U_1+G_2U_2}$ 这一对方程。这就是第 5 章的星形-三角形变换：T 是星形，$\pi$ 是三角形。用 Z 参数描述自然引出 T 形网络，用 Y 参数描述则引出与之对偶的 $\pi$ 形网络。反过来，每个 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Z_{12}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{Z_{21}}$ 的二端口网络都有一个不含电源的 T 形等效电路和一个 $\pi$ 形等效电路（第 10 阶段）。

例

$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\det\mathbf Z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{35\cdot40\,\Omega^2-20\cdot20\,\Omega^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1000\,\Omega^2}$，所以

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathbf Y}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\frac{1}{1000\,\Omega^2}\begin{pmatrix}40&-20\\-20&35\end{pmatrix}\Omega}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\begin{pmatrix}40&-20\\-20&35\end{pmatrix}\mathrm{mS},}$$

这正是短路试验的结果。$\pi$ 形网络的 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G_2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{20}$ mS，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G_1}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{40\,\mathrm{mS}-20\,\mathrm{mS}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{20}$ mS，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G_3}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{15}$ mS，即 50 $\Omega$、50 $\Omega$ 和 66.7 $\Omega$。接同样的电源和负载，它同样给出 7.75 V 和 3 V。

⁠极限情况：并非每种形式都存在。串联电阻 $R$ 和并联电阻 $R$ 单独位于两个端口之间时，分别有

$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathbf Y_\text{串联}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{G\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix},}$$
$$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathbf Z_\text{并联}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{R\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.}$$

两个行列式都为零。串联电阻没有 Z 形式：这时总有 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{I_2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-I_1}$，电流不能自由选择，所以不能作为原因。并联电阻没有 Y 形式，因为总有 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_1}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{U_2}$。理想电流控制电流源也是如此：它的电流是刚性耦合的。

问题为什么 $Y_{11}$ 为正，$Y_{21}$ 却为负？

这是参考箭头造成的。$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{U_2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$ 时，端口 1 上的正电压驱动电流流入，$Y_{11}>0$。这个电流从另一个端口流出，但计数方向是流入。因此 T 形、$\pi$ 形等无源电路的转移电导为负。

练习 6 ★ 去与回

一个二端口网络有 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Z_{11}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{20\,\Omega}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Z_{12}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{Z_{21}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{10\,\Omega}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Z_{22}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{55\,\Omega}$。

  1. 计算 $\mathbf Y$。

  2. 求 T 形网络和 $\pi$ 形网络的各电阻。

  3. 写出 100 $\Omega$ 串联电阻的 $\mathbf Y$。为什么 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\mathbf Z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{\mathbf Y^{-1}}$ 行不通？

解答

(a) $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\det\mathbf Z}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{20\cdot55\,\Omega^2-10\cdot10\,\Omega^2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{1000\,\Omega^2}$，所以 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Y_{11}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{55}$ mS，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Y_{12}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{Y_{21}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-10}$ mS，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Y_{22}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{20}$ mS。 (b) T：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{R_\mathrm{b}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{10\,\Omega}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{R_\mathrm{a}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{10\,\Omega}$，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{R_\mathrm{c}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{45\,\Omega}$。$\pi$：$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G_2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{10}$ mS，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G_1}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{45}$ mS，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{G_3}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{10}$ mS，即串联 100 $\Omega$，并联 22.2 $\Omega$ 和 100 $\Omega$。 (c) $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Y_{11}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{Y_{22}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{10}$ mS，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{Y_{12}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{Y_{21}}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-10}$ mS，$\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{\det\mathbf Y}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{0}$。逆矩阵不存在，因为总有 $\textcolor{#1b7f4a}{I_2}\textcolor{#15633a}{=}\textcolor{#c2410c}{-I_1}$，电流不能自由选择。
